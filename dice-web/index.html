      How to check this roll

      1. The roll request fixed a pre-seed. Mixed with the hash of the block it was requested in, it gives the seed. Nobody could know that block hash in advance.
      2. The oracle answered with a VRF proof for that seed. The coordinator contract checked the proof against the oracle's public key on-chain, in the proof transaction. A wrong or invented number would have made that transaction fail.
      3. The proof gives the VRF output. The random word is keccak256(abi.encode(output, 0)), and the die shows word mod 6 + 1. Your browser recomputed both below.
