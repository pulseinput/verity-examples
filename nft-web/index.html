Why it's fair

  • The Orb is minted before its randomness exists, so a minter can't look at the traits and back out.
  • The random number depends on a future block hash, so nobody knows it at mint time.
  • Verity proves the number on-chain, and the contract reveals each Orb exactly once. There is no reroll.
  • The art and metadata are generated by the contract from the stored traits, so they can't be swapped later.
